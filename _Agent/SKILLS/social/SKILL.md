Aliases: social, content, Melina, brand, post, analytics, partnership

## Purpose

Maintain Melina's social media planning, content calendar, analytics, and brand partnership tracking.

## Run Steps

1. Read `08_Social_Media_Orlando_Theme_Park_Family/README.md`.
2. Put content ideas in `post_ideas.md`.
3. Put scheduled or planned posts in `content_calendar.csv`.
4. Put performance data in `analytics_log.csv`.
5. Put partnership opportunities and deliverables in `brand_partnerships.csv`.
6. Add due dates or posting dates to the calendar when useful.

## Dry Run

Show the content, analytics, or partnership rows that would be added.

## Safety

Do not publish posts, send brand messages, accept deals, or disclose private analytics externally without explicit user confirmation.
