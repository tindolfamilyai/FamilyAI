# Skill: Kids Records

Aliases: kids, child, Bella, Mila, pediatrician, vaccine, growth, daycare, school

## Purpose

Maintain child profiles, medical logs, growth logs, and related calendar items.

## Run Steps

1. Read `03_Kids/README.md`.
2. Choose `03_Kids/Bella/` or `03_Kids/Mila/`.
3. Put medical visits, vaccines, illness, and follow-up notes in `medical_log.csv`.
4. Put height, weight, clothing size, shoe size, and milestone records in `growth_log.csv`.
5. Put durable context in `profile.md`.
6. Add dated appointments to `00_Calendar/YYYY-MM.csv` when date and time are known.

## Dry Run

Show the child folder, log file, row, and calendar entry that would be created.

## Safety

Treat child information as sensitive. Do not make medical decisions.
