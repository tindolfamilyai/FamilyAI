# Skill: Meal Planning

Aliases: meals, grocery, groceries, recipe, dinner, lunch, breakfast

## Purpose

Plan meals, update grocery needs, preserve reusable recipes, and log nutrition in a way that supports Blake and Melina's body-composition goals.

## Run Steps

1. Read `02_Health_Fitness/README.md`.
2. Read `02_Health_Fitness/nutrition_goals.md`.
3. Read `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/weekly_nutrition_targets.md`.
4. Read `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/current_week.md`, `meal_plan.csv`, `grocery_list.csv`, and `recipes.md`.
5. Update `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/current_week.md` for the human-readable plan.
6. Update `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/meal_plan.csv` for dated meals.
7. Update `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/grocery_list.csv` for needed ingredients.
8. Add durable recipe notes to `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/recipes.md`.

## Macro-Aware Planning

- Blake target: about 2,750-2,950 calories/day and 215g protein/day.
- Melina target: about 1,700-1,900 calories/day and 130-145g protein/day.
- Plan one shared family dinner, then vary portions or add-ons for Blake, Melina, Bella, and Mila.
- Support adult protein targets with realistic meals and grocery staples.
- Do not assign calorie, macro, or weight-loss targets to Bella or Mila.

## Food Logging

When asked to log food:

1. Append daily meal entries to `02_Health_Fitness/Meal_Plans/food_log.csv`.
2. Use rough estimates when exact nutrition is unknown, and mark them in `Notes`.
3. Log weekly weigh-ins, waist notes, energy, hunger, and training notes in `body_metrics_log.csv`.
4. Use `body_metrics_log.csv` and `food_log.csv` for 2-3 week reviews before recommending target changes.

## Dry Run

Show the meal plan, grocery rows, food-log rows, body-metric rows, and recipe notes that would be added.

## Safety

Respect allergy, medical, and child preference notes in family profiles if present. This is planning and tracking support, not medical advice.
